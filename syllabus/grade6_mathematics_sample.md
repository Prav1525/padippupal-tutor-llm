## Topic: Fractions

A fraction represents equal parts of a whole. It is written as a/b, where a is the numerator and b is the denominator. The denominator must not be zero.

Example: If a pizza is divided into 4 equal slices and 1 slice is taken, the taken part is 1/4. The numerator is 1 and the denominator is 4.

Equivalent fractions represent the same value. For example, 1/2 and 2/4 are equivalent because multiplying both numerator and denominator of 1/2 by 2 gives 2/4.

To compare fractions with the same denominator, compare their numerators. For example, 3/8 is greater than 2/8 because 3 > 2.

## Teaching guidance

- Start with a concrete example such as a paper folded into equal parts.
- Explain one idea at a time and use age-appropriate words.
- Ask one short understanding-check question.
